7. Game Mechanics

  • Permadeath on Save Slots: You have 10 slots to save games manually or automatically. Autosave happens silently every 5 seconds. By design and game mechanic, if your ship is destroyed during a run in progress, the associated save slot will be automatically and irreversibly deleted.
  • Flight Diplomas: The mission certificate and diploma are generated locally using Canvas graphics processing in your browser. Saving and sharing the resulting image runs locally through your device's native operating system functions (Web Share API).
  • Automatic life regeneration: Lives regenerate automatically over time, even with the game closed, up to a maximum of 10. Interval: 5 minutes in Normal and Zigzag mode, 2 minutes in Fast mode. Offline time accumulates and is calculated when you reopen the game. You can also restore lives instantly in the Shop with virtual currencies.
  • Portable export: The backup .csv file is encrypted with AES-GCM and is portable across installations; it does not contain or restore your pilot nickname, which remains local and random per installation.

10. World Leaderboard Data and Record Permanence

When you submit a score to the world Top 100, the following data is stored and publicly visible:

  • Pilot nickname: Randomly assigned by the game (public)
  • Score: Your final score in points (public)
  • Game mode: Normal, Fast or Swingcopter (implicit in the table)
  • Run duration: Survival time (public, used for anti-cheat verification)
  • Aggregated flight stats: Asteroids destroyed and power-ups collected (public)

Internally, the system also stores technical fields score_actual and tiempo in Firestore for anti-cheat validation (mathematical cross-check between score and duration). No personal data, email addresses or account identifiers are published. Scores remain on the leaderboard while inside its Top 100; however, you may request at any time the erasure or correction of a score associated with your installation by writing to the contact email indicated in these Terms.

11. Privacy Control and Data Management

  • Disable Leaderboard: You can disable score submission to the world leaderboard at any time from Settings → Privacy. If disabled, no scores will be sent and the game stays local, but it will still make HTTPS requests for news and time sync and will technically expose your IP to Google.
  • Export: You can download an encrypted .csv file (AES-GCM, portable key) with a backup of your local progress and save slots from Settings. The file is portable across devices/browsers and does not include your pilot nickname.
  • Import: You can restore data from a previously exported file.
  • Delete Local Data: You can erase all your local data —including your world leaderboard scores in all modes— at any time from Settings. This action wipes your local progress and automatically removes your Top 100 entries; it is irreversible both locally and on the leaderboard.
