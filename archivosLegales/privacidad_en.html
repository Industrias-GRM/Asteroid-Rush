Privacy Policy of Asteroid Rush

Last updated: September 2, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what data the "Asteroid Rush" extension collects and how it is processed and protected in this BETA phase.

1. Information We Collect

  • Local Data (LocalStorage): Your accumulated game progress, volume and video settings, cosmetic skin inventory, unlocked achievements record, active save slots and currency balances are stored exclusively on your device using standard browser technology. This data never leaves your computer unless you export a backup yourself.
  • Random Pilot Nickname: The game automatically assigns a random identifier (for example, "Astronauta4821") generated and stored locally in your browser. You do not choose this nickname and it contains no information about you. It is only published alongside your score if you decide to submit a score to the world ranking.
  • Public Ranking Data (Top 100): When you submit a score to the world leaderboard, your assigned random nickname, achieved score, survival time, flight statistics (asteroids destroyed and power-ups collected) and the active game mode are publicly recorded.
  • User Accounts: The registered account system is currently disabled. We do not collect email addresses or passwords. If this feature is reactivated in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand.
  • Time Synchronisation: To enable temporary promotions (Happy Hour 20:00–21:00), the game synchronises the time once per day with Google Firestore servers via the HTTP Date header. As with any HTTPS connection, your IP address is technically transmitted to Google to establish the connection; we do not collect or store it, we only use the response Date timestamp to calculate monotonic server time. The resulting value is stored exclusively on your device.
  • News System: The game fetches news published by the developer from a Firebase Firestore project. News items are cached locally for 24 hours to reduce network usage. As with any HTTPS request, your IP is technically exposed to Google/Firebase for delivery; we do not send additional identifiers nor store your IP.
  • IP Address (unavoidable technical data): Any connection to the leaderboard, news or time sync entails transmitting your IP to Google as infrastructure provider. This is an incidental technical personal data that the provider may log per its privacy policy; we do not extract or retain it.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the processing described above relies on:

  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR): for the technical operation of the world ranking, cheat prevention and moderation of published content.
  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR): submitting scores to the leaderboard is entirely optional and can be disabled at any time from Settings → Privacy. While enabled, submission only occurs when you finish a run with a score that enters the Top 100.
  • Local storage of your progress takes place on your own device under your control and does not constitute processing by us that could be revoked.

3. How We Use Your Information and Administrative Access

  • Public Competition: Displaying your position and flight performance in the world Top 100 table, which is publicly accessible to the whole player community.
  • Competitive Integrity: Applying automatic anti-cheat filters (mathematical cross-check between score and run duration).
  • Administrative Access and Moderation: Administrators hold technical privileges to access the leaderboard database, strictly limited to service maintenance tasks, user support, security audits and removal of inappropriate content.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

For the online systems to function we use a single provider:

  • Google Firebase / Firestore: Hosts the world leaderboard database and game news, on servers located within the European Economic Area (Europe). Processing by Google is governed by the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission, incorporated into the Google Cloud/Firebase terms of service.

We do not use any intermediary server of our own nor additional third-party services for data processing.

5. International Data Transfers

Data submitted to the leaderboard is stored on servers located in the European Union. We do not carry out international transfers of personal data to third countries.

6. Data Retention

  • Leaderboard scores: Kept while they remain within their mode's Top 100; once out they are automatically replaced in the live Top, but historical copies are stored internally and will be shown publicly in the future History section of the leaderboard (coming soon) —only nickname, score and mode, without duration or stats.
  • Local data: Kept until you delete the game data or uninstall the extension.
  • Score Removal (BETA): The developer reserves the right to remove leaderboard scores detected as fraudulent, tampered with, or generated through cheats, bots or exploits, following technical verification. General leaderboard resets may also be carried out in the event of critical updates, seasonal changes or transition to a stable version, with reasonable notice when possible.

7. Your Rights and Control

Pursuant to Articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR, free of charge you may exercise the following rights by writing to the contact email: access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction of processing and portability. We will respond without undue delay and no later than 1 month from your request, extendable by 2 additional months where complexity or the number of requests requires it (we will inform you within the first month of the extension and its reason).

You also have these direct controls inside the game:

  • Disable Leaderboard: You can turn off score submission at any time from Settings → Privacy. If disabled, no scores will be sent and the game stays local, but it will still make HTTPS requests for news and time sync and will technically expose your IP to Google.
  • Local Export & Import: You can download a .csv file encrypted (AES-GCM) with your progress and save slots, and import it later.
  • Delete Local Data: You can wipe all local storage from Settings —this also automatically and irreversibly deletes your world leaderboard scores in all modes, both locally and on the server.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es) if you believe the processing does not comply with current regulations.

8. Minimum Age

You must be at least 14 years old, or the minimum age required by the law of your country to use digital services of this type, if higher.

9. Prohibited Conduct and API Use

  • API Key Abuse: Extracting or using the credentials included in the code for external requests, spam or mass data extraction is strictly prohibited.
  • Using cheats, exploits, bots or any third-party software to modify the game experience.
  • Attempting to interrupt or overload the leaderboard or news servers.

We reserve the right to remove any score we deem fraudulent or inappropriate, without prior notice.

You are advised to review it periodically, especially before submitting scores. Continued use after the effective date implies acceptance; where the change requires new consent (Art. 7 GDPR), we will request it expressly and you may withdraw prior consent without retroactive effect.
